1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Websites use them to work properly, to stay secure, and to remember your choices. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage and session storage, work in much the same way, and this policy covers them too.

2. Our approach

We keep cookies to a minimum. By default, the Website uses only the cookies it needs to work securely and to remember your cookie choices. Any analytics or marketing tools we use are listed under Analytics and marketing tools at the end of this policy. They stay switched off until you consent to them in the cookie banner, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

4. Cookie categories in our cookie banner

  • Strictly necessary: always on, because the Website cannot work securely without them. These are the cookies listed above.
  • Functionality, Tracking, and Targeting and advertising: optional categories used only with your consent. The tools in each category, if any, are listed under Analytics and marketing tools below and in the cookie banner.

5. Managing your choices

  • Consent Preferences: you can change your choices at any time using the round cookie icon in the bottom-left corner of every page.
  • Your browser: most browsers let you view, block and delete cookies. If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the Website, such as forms and signing in, will not work.
